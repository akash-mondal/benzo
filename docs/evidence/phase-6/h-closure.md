## README honest-limits patch list (ready to paste)

History: each sentence below was a README GAP found by B38. All are now in README `## Honest limits` (R86; rank 16 and rank 22 reworded in R87), so no README GAP remains.
Paste into `## Honest limits` ("Stated again for this build:", after line 139 or at the section
end); the head applies them.

1. **Rank 6 (066):** The board-witness set is chosen in the signed manifest, so its honesty is an
   assumption: the one-root-per-auction rule stops a witness quorum from certifying two different
   frozen roots, but not from certifying one wrong root. Choose `q_w` so no quorum can be built
   from witnesses you do not trust.
2. **Rank 7 (017):** dropped by the head (R86): repo history, handled by the release scrub.
3. **Rank 9 (037):** `benzo fund quote` sends your quote fields (origin-chain refund address,
   your shielded UA, the round amount) to the NEAR 1Click service. The auction never calls it,
   and the default is shielded ZEC you already hold.
4. **Rank 10 (038):** Nothing in Benzo bridges funds straight to an auction deposit; if you send
   transparent or bridge proceeds directly to a deposit address, the source chain and the bridge
   can link them to your bid. Fund your own shielded wallet first, wait for confirmations, then
   deposit the fixed C. Benzo cannot detect a direct bridge payment — a payer is unobservable on
   shielded Zcash.
5. **Rank 16 (015), as reworded in R87:** A malicious coordinator can redirect the seller's proceeds: signer hosts
   check the seller payout's amount, not its address, and the published settlement does not name the recipient.
   Fix before mainnet (H10, P1).
6. **Rank 19 (052):** Signer-share and envelope-key backups are each operator's own
   responsibility — Benzo ships no encrypted backup. A lost share can stall settlement; a stolen
   share counts toward the 2-of-3.
7. **Rank 32 (067):** There is no lost-key recovery: a bidder who loses a bid's one-time owner
   key cannot claim its refund (the row stays an exception and nobody may redirect it).
