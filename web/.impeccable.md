## Design Context

### Users
- Zecathon judges (Private Markets track), opening the site cold during judging (Oct 29 – Nov 22). They have minutes, a laptop, maybe a Zashi testnet wallet. They must see within seconds that the markets are real and live on Zcash testnet, and be able to bid in one.
- Sellers and buyers of the organiser's world (zkSNARKs / Zilkroad auctions and buybacks): people who already run sealed and live auctions in ZEC and would use this on Monday.
- Bidders: they seal a price in the browser, pay a deposit from their own shielded wallet, and later check that their bid was counted and refunded correctly.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: sealed, shining, provable.
- The page is a night sky of objects. Every object is something real: an auction that ran on testnet, or one piece of how a sealed bid stays sealed. Nothing is decoration only.
- Emotional goal: curiosity first (drag, find, click), then trust (every number links to a public, verifiable record).

### Aesthetic Direction
- Fixed by the user: an exact replica of svgdoodles.com, re-made for Benzo. Near-black field #0a0a0f, chrome-banded doodles drawn by a three.js shader, drifting dust and the odd shooting star, a white pill label on hover, and the small "file ticket" card (folded corner, debossed title, inset screen with grid and tick marks, status light, perforation with notches, format picker and Copy).
- Type as on the reference: Geist (text), Geist Mono (the card's small labels), Bricolage Grotesque extra-bold (debossed titles), Georgia serif for the chrome words floating in the field.
- Theme: dark only. Judges open it at a desk; the reference is dark; the chrome reads only on near-black.
- Anti-references: crypto dashboards (neon, gradients, glass cards), generic "AI landing pages" (centred hero, three icon cards), any fake numbers.

### Design Principles
1. Every doodle is a real thing: an auction with its live state from testnet, or a mechanism with the command that runs it.
2. The card is the interface. Inner pages are built from the same parts: screens, tickets, perforations, status lights, debossed titles.
3. Nothing invented: states, prices, counts and checks come from the published auction documents and `benzo verify`.
4. The bid never leaves the browser in the clear: sealing runs the shipped sealer bundle unchanged.
5. Keyboard and reduced-motion users get the same content: a list view of every doodle, and still chrome when motion is reduced.

### Deliberate departures from house rules (user instruction: exact replica)
- Monospace small tracked uppercase labels on the card (ACTIVE, category, Copy) are part of the reference and are kept.
- The card keeps its single inset shadow and its folded-corner gradient chip as on the reference.
